# Grammar Content

## Scope and editorial choices

The app contains 80 original review lessons: the existing 20 A1 lessons and 12 new lessons at each of A2, B1, B2, C1 and C2. A1 remains in `../app/grammar.json`; the new material is in `../app/grammar-levels.json`. No grammar PDF is included in this edition.

Every new lesson contains an English explanation, a form/pattern table, at least two German examples with English meanings, pitfalls, and a practice prompt with a suggested answer. Suggested answers are not always the only correct formulations. C2 concentrates on precise interpretation, discourse and register as well as complex grammar; it is not a claim that these constructions occur only at C2.

The CEFR describes language competence rather than supplying this app's topic allocation. Levels here are editorial study groupings, not a certified course, a complete examination syllabus, or a reproduction of a publisher's textbook. Explanations and examples were authored for this app. Automatic rendering/content checks do not constitute an independent language-teacher review.

## Reference checks

The following primary references were consulted for level framing and selected difficult constructions. They are references, not sources of copied lesson passages or complete validations of every lesson:

- [Goethe-Institut: CEFR levels](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/uun/dln/ger.html): competence-based framing.
- [Goethe-Institut: C2](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/uun/dln/dc2.html): advanced precision and command of language.
- [IDS grammis: Ersatzinfinitiv](https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik/1615): modal verbs in compound forms.
- [IDS grammis: original utterance and indirect reporting](https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik/643): reporting perspective and changes in reference.
- [IDS grammis: mood and indirect-reporting questions](https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/fragenkategorie/25): indicative, Konjunktiv and replacement forms.
- [Goethe-Institut: C1 modal meanings](https://lernen.goethe.de/deutschonline/C1/PDF/DTonlineC1_K10_GR-RM_Rueckschau_DE.pdf): attributed claims and modal interpretations.
